## Analysis of Saber 11 results

- **Type:** research / data science · **Org:** Hubrain
- **Summary:** We created an ML model to predict the results of Colombia's national standardized test (Saber 11) from the socioeconomic characteristics of the students. The main goal was to measure the extent to which a student's socioeconomic status determines their performance on standardized tests, a question closely related to the meritocracy fallacy.
- **Code:** https://gitlab.com/hubrain/ICFES-SocioEconomico
- **Note:** the linked "meritocracy fallacy" Notion page no longer exists.

## Analysis of the Covid-19 pandemic in Colombia

- **Type:** research / publication · **Org:** Hubrain
- **Summary:** We studied the under-reporting of Covid cases in Colombia and estimated the true scale of the pandemic. We improved the previously reported algorithm by accounting for the specifics of Colombian demographics and the relation between age groups and Covid-19 sensitivity.
- **Paper (medRxiv):** https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2020.12.30.20249052v1
- **Code:** https://gitlab.com/hubrain/covid19

## Analysis of student comments (opinion mining)

- **Type:** research / publication (ICAI 2020)
- **Summary:** We analysed students' comments in class reviews, used NLP methods to identify latent topics, and designed a data processing pipeline to automatically identify bad reviews and their topics.
- **Paper:** https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-030-61702-8_23

## Simulation of multi-lane traffic dynamics

- **Type:** B.Sc. thesis (Physics)
- **Summary:** We developed high-performance code to simulate multi-lane systems with car interactions and performed statistical analysis on two-lane systems. Results were presented at Traffic and Granular Flow (Navarra, 2019).
- **Thesis:** https://drive.google.com/file/d/1iW4wcanFF1jaBMap7b8ekN22cE6Hvyzb/view
- **Code:** https://github.com/juancitotrucupey/Depured_Multilane

## The Non-Commutative Brillouin torus

- **Type:** M.Sc. thesis (Mathematics)
- **Summary:** We study the Non-Commutative Brillouin torus and its applications in solid state physics, focusing on its structure as a non-commutative smooth manifold and on how K-theory and cyclic cohomology provide topological invariants for that non-commutative space — results that belong to non-commutative differential topology.
- **Blog post:** see `blog/non-commutative-brillouin-torus.md`
- **Thesis:** https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1VXjomd_6Oim7oigK2n3sZYwsrhWClumS
